Field reference - what to write

One person per block. Put the name on its own line first, then labelled lines. Blank line starts the next person. A line of --- also separates people.

  • Born: Died: Buried: Christened: Married: Residence: Emigrated: Occupation: - followed by a date, a place, or both separated by a comma.
  • Father: Mother: Spouse: Children: Also known as: - followed by names.
  • Source: (or Sources:, Evidence:) - followed by the citation. Separate multiple citations with a semicolon.
  • Conflict: - anything the sources disagree about.
  • Note: - background that is not a claim.

Dates can be 12 March 1872, March 12, 1872, 1872, c. 1872, before 1875, between 1860 and 1870 or 1940s. Places are read as Town, County, State or Parish, County, England.
